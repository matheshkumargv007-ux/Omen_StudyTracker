STUDY TIMER + DEEP-FOCUS STREAK

FEATURES
- Focus: 50 or 25 minutes.
- Short break: 10 or 5 minutes.
- Long break: 15 or 10 minutes.
- Cycle: Focus → Short break → Focus → Short break → Focus → Long break.
- Start/Pause, Skip, Restart.
- Deep-focus streak table: tracks daily focus time and whether you reach at least 2 hours.
- Automatically checks off OmenTracker's Deep work session habit after the daily goal is reached, when both apps share the same browser origin and the same browser.

IMPORTANT FOR CONNECTING WITH OMENTRACKER
Browser storage is isolated by website origin. For automatic sync, host Study Timer and OmenTracker under the SAME HTTPS origin (for example, the same username.github.io domain in separate folders), and open both in the same browser profile. If the apps are on different domains, browsers do not allow direct localStorage sharing.

HOW TO PREVIEW
Open index.html on a computer. For app installation and offline caching, host the app over HTTPS.
